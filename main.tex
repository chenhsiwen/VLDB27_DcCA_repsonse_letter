\title{\textbf{Response to Reviewer Comments}\\
\large ``Towards Data-Centric Counterfactual Analysis'' [Vision] (Paper ID 167)}

\author{Bryce Bern, Hsi-Wen Chen, Ming-Syan Chen, Tianlong Chen, and Jian Pei}

\maketitle

We sincerely thank the meta-reviewer and all reviewers for their constructive and insightful feedback. By addressing those comments, we substantially improve our paper. We are encouraged that the reviewers find the vision ``timely and an important problem'' with appreciated ``novelty and the potential impact.'' In this document, we first respond to the five revision items specified in the meta-review, then address the weak points and detailed-evaluation points individually raised by each reviewer. All changes in the revised manuscript are highlighted in {\color{blue}blue}.

To substantiate the empirical claims, we conducted \textbf{a new set of experiments} that extend our runtime study (Table~1) with counterfactual \emph{quality}: collective-validity objectives (diversity, robustness, fairness), a validity/feasibility comparison against a model-centric counterfactual method, an exact-vs-approximate retrieval study, and a scalability study. These experiments are reproducible (numpy, fixed seeds) and are summarized in Tables~\ref{tab:quality}--\ref{tab:scale} in this document.
